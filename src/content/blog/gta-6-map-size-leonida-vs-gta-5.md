When Rockstar Games released the debut trailer for *Grand Theft Auto VI*, it officially introduced players to the fictitious southeastern state of **Leonida**—an exaggerated parody of Florida centered around a neon-lit, modern-day Vice City.

Ever since, players have debated the exact landmass scale of Leonida compared to previous Rockstar titles. Through meticulous cartography by the global GTA VI community mapping project, we now have verifiable spatial models measuring Leonida's physical dimensions.

> **AEO Executive Summary:** The GTA 6 Leonida map spans an estimated 125 square kilometers, making it roughly 2.5 times larger than GTA 5's San Andreas and 66% larger than Red Dead Redemption 2.

## The Community Mapping Project: How Coordinates Proved Map Scale

Unlike speculative rumors, the estimates for Leonida's physical boundaries stem from mathematical evidence found in the 2022 developer leak.

During internal testing, developer cameras rendered on-screen 3D world coordinates `(X, Y, Z)`. By plotting camera focal lengths against confirmed landmark positions—such as the Ocean Drive neon strip, the Port Gellhorn racetrack, and Hamlet—cartographers mapped spatial distances across hundreds of overlapping points.

Key findings confirmed by the coordinates include:
1. **Distance from North to South:** Spans more than 16,000 in-game units, exceeding the maximum vertical span of Los Santos from the southern docks to Paleto Bay.
2. **Waterway Depth:** Unlike GTA 5, which placed mountains in the interior, Leonida surrounds waterways, marshlands, and intra-coastal canals that allow full aquatic circumnavigation.

## Key Regions and Confirmed Biomes in Leonida

Leonida is not just an expanded Vice City; it features diverse geographic ecosystems:

* **Vice City Metro:** Vibrant nightlife, high-rise luxury hotels, South Beach Art Deco architecture, and affluent financial districts.
* **Port Gellhorn:** A decaying, post-industrial port town on the western coast with abandoned motels, strip malls, and working docks.
* **Grassrivers:** The Everglades equivalent, featuring dense wetlands, airboats, wild alligators, and murky estuaries.
* **Ambrosia & Mount Kalaga:** The northern agricultural heartland containing rural farms, dirt raceways, trailer parks, and hilly terrain.
* **The Keys (Gellhorn Keys):** A chain of tropical coral cays connected by long overseas highways, luxury marinas, and diving spots.

## Interior Accessibility: Quality Over Pure Quantity

While raw square kilometers matter, what sets GTA 6 apart is **interior density**. In GTA 5, the vast majority of buildings were static, non-enterable solid blocks. 

In GTA 6, leaks and trailers confirmed that convenience stores, diners, pawn shops, subway stations, hotels, and suburban homes have fully textured interiors with working security cameras and lootable cash registers, transforming urban traversal into an interactive heist playground.
