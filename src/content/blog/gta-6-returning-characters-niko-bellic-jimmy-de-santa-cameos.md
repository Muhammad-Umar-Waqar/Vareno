Rockstar Games has a rich tradition of connecting the criminal underworlds of Liberty City, Los Santos, and Vice City across its HD Universe. In ***Grand Theft Auto VI***, players have uncovered astonishing cameos from beloved franchise icons: **Niko Bellic** (*Grand Theft Auto IV*) and **Jimmy De Santa** (*Grand Theft Auto V*).

Here is the full breakdown of how these legacy characters return in Leonida, where you can find them, who voices them, and the story secrets behind Jason Duval’s age and military background.

Eighteen years after stepping off the *Platypus* cargo freighter in Liberty City, **Niko Bellic** returns to the franchise in a pivotal, brooding Chapter 5 cutscene:

* **The Encounter:** During the mission *"Taking Liberties"*, Jason and Lucia are summoned to an offshore marina warehouse to meet an international intermediary connected to Eastern European shipping lines.
* **His Role:** Niko appears heavily aged in his early 50s, wearing a dark leather jacket over a faded sweater. While no longer the hungry street gun for hire from 2008, he now operates as a quiet, lethal syndicate enforcer brokering port logistics in Vice City.
* **Rockstar's Secret Credits:** To preserve the surprise, Rockstar Games listed actor **Michael Hollick** in early review rollouts under the pseudonym *"The Hitman"*, though fans and voice comparisons immediately identified his unmistakable gravelly voice.
