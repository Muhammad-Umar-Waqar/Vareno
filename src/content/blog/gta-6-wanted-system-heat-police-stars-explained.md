The law enforcement system in *Grand Theft Auto VI* represents the most ambitious artificial intelligence overhaul in Rockstar Games history. Moving far beyond *GTA 5*’s telepathic police spawns, *GTA 6* introduces an ultra-realistic **6-Star Wanted System** driven by **Heat recognition vectors**, CCTV traffic cameras, physical witness investigations, and forensic evidence tracking.

Whether you're robbing a roadside convenience store in Port Gellhorn or running contraband through Vice Beach, understanding how the police track you is critical to survival.

## 2. Understanding Star Colors: Patrol, Search & Pursuit

The mini-map HUD in *GTA 6* provides real-time situational awareness through dynamic star coloration:

* **Yellow / Amber Stars (Witness Investigation):** A citizen or shopkeeper dialed 911 or tripped a silent alarm. The police do not know your exact face yet; units are dispatched to investigate the crime scene.
* **Flashing Red & Blue Stars (Active Pursuit):** Officers have direct line-of-sight on you. Lethal engagement is authorized, and tactical vehicles will actively ram your car.
* **Solid White / Gray Stars (Searching & Lost Contact):** You broke line-of-sight. Police maintain a search radius around your last-known position. If you remain still in an alley or garage, the meter ticks down.

Unlike older games where escaping a circular radar zone instantly cleared your record, *GTA 6* introduces **persistent forensic Heat**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    GTA 6 HEAT RECOGNITION VECTORS           │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Vehicle Heat        │ Plate, make, model & color flagged    │
│ Clothing Heat       │ Jacket, shirt & pants logged by CCTV  │
│ Facial Heat         │ Unmasked facial profile broadcasted   │
│ Couple Heat         │ Duo suspect alert (Lucia + Jason)     │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Vehicle Heat (Car Description)
If police or witnesses see your vehicle, its license plate, model, and color are transmitted to dispatch. Even if you lose line-of-sight, passing a police cruiser in the same vehicle will immediately trigger an arrest attempt. You must respray the car at a Pay 'n' Spray or steal an untracked vehicle.

### 2. Clothing & CCTV Heat
Traffic intersections, highway tolls, and store interiors feature functional CCTV surveillance cameras. If you commit an armed robbery without a bandana or balaclava, cameras log your outfit. Changing shirts, jackets, or removing hats at a clothing shop or vehicle trunk drops your clothing heat immediately.

### 3. Couple Heat (Lucia & Jason Duo)
When Lucia and Jason commit high-profile robberies together, dispatch broadcasts a bulletin for a **male and female criminal duo**. When traveling in the same car, law enforcement alertness is doubled. Splitting up into separate vehicles or entering safehouses breaks the couple tracking link.

## 4. How to Outsmart the Police in Leonida

To evade high-level pursuits in *GTA 6*, players must use tactical environmental awareness:

1. **Ditch the Car Early:** Abandon hot vehicles behind warehouses or under highway bridges.
2. **Swap Clothes at Your Trunk:** Keep extra jackets, hoodies, and masks stored in your vehicle trunk (inspired by *Red Dead Redemption 2*'s horse inventory system).
3. **Avoid Main Highway Tolls & Traffic Cams:** Interstates like the Vice City Expressway have automated plate scanners. Stick to back-alleys, dirt trails, and canals.
4. **Use Leonida’s Natural Swamps:** Police cruisers cannot navigate deep mud or mangrove wetlands without heavy all-terrain vehicles, giving players time to escape via airboats or jet skis.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you surrender to the police in GTA 6?
Yes. At 1 and 2 stars, putting away your weapon and standing still allows you to surrender peacefully, resulting in a brief holding cell cutscene and a confiscation fine rather than a wasted screen.

### Do police call for backup realistically?
Yes. In the 2022 leaks, officers demonstrated realistic radio animations where they waited for partner units before breaching buildings or entering dark alleys.

### Can you shoot out CCTV cameras?
Yes. Silenced pistols can disable security cameras and traffic lights, preventing your identity and license plate from being logged by police networks.
