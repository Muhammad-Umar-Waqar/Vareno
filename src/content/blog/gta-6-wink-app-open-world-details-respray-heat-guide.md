## 1. How the Wink (WAINK) Car App Works in GTA 6

In previous GTA titles, stealing any car was as simple as pressing the enter button. In *GTA 6*, high-end modern vehicles feature realistic anti-theft security systems, requiring the **WAINK App**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 WAINK APP VEHICLE SCANNER                   │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Access Method       │ Up D-Pad -> Smartphone -> WAINK Icon │
│ Primary Function    │ Remote vehicle telemetry & security   │
│ Scanned Metrics     │ Alarm type, immobilizer, GPS tracker  │
│ Tool Requirements   │ Slim Jim, OBD Bypass Tool, Signal Dup │
│ Chop Shop Valuation │ Instant fence payout calculation      │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### How to Scan and Steal Cars Using WAINK:
1. **Target a Vehicle:** Stand within 15 meters of a parked car and open your phone with `Up D-Pad`. Select the yellow **WAINK** app icon.
2. **Scan Security Protocols:** Aim your phone at the car. The app displays whether the vehicle has an active alarm, a satellite GPS tracker, or a computerized keyless immobilizer.
3. **Use the Required Tool:**
   * **Older Sedans / Trucks:** Require a simple **Slim Jim** or window punch.
   * **Modern Sports Cars:** Require an **OBD Port Bypass unit** plugged into the steering column to crack the electronic ECU.
   * **Exotic Supercars:** Require a **Signal Duplicator** to clone the owner's key fob from nearby pedestrians.
4. **Disable GPS Trackers:** Once inside, use the WAINK mini-game to locate and snip the concealed GPS transponder behind the dashboard before police dispatch triangulates your coordinates.

## 2. Vehicle Respray Tactics: Losing Police Heat (The Phoenix Trick)

A viral gameplay guide by creator ZLaner demonstrated how players can escape aggressive 3-star and 4-star police chases by utilizing **vehicle respray mechanics**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  VEHICLE HEAT EVASION RULES                 │
├───────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Active Line-of-   │ Respray DISABLED. Garages will not open │
│ Sight Pursuit     │ while cruisers are directly behind you. │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Broken Line-of-   │ Respray ACTIVE. Painting your car       │
│ Sight (Gray Stars)│ completely wipes Vehicle Heat.          │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Retained Heat     │ Facial & Clothing Heat remain active.   │
│ Warning           │ Change clothes to be 100% clean.        │
└───────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Step-by-Step Evasion Trick:
1. When driving a recognizable muscle car like the **Imponte Phoenix**, police broadcast your car model, color, and license plate.
2. Cut into highway underpasses, multi-level parking garages, or dirt canals to break line-of-sight until the mini-map stars turn **solid gray** (Search Mode).
3. Pull into any local **Customs / Pay 'n' Spray** garage. 
4. Select a new primary paint color and request new license plates.
5. Exit the garage. Police cruisers cruising the neighborhood will drive right past your resprayed vehicle without triggering an alert—provided you keep driving at a normal speed and don't commit new traffic infractions.

## 3. Dynamic Unscripted Open-World Events: Plane Crashes & Wild NPCs

Leonida features the most reactive and unpredictable artificial intelligence ever placed in a gaming sandbox:

### Unscripted Everglades Plane Crashes
While navigating the inland wetlands and swamps outside Vice City, players can look up to witness dynamic environmental disasters. Twin-engine smuggling aircraft or private luxury jets can suffer engine fires and violently crash through mangrove trees. 
* Responding emergency services (search-and-rescue helicopters and Coast Guard airboats) spawn naturally.
* Players can dive into the wreckage to scavenge sunken drug crates, cash duffel bags, and rare military firearms before authorities arrive.

### Reactive Pedestrian & Road Rage AI
* **Unpredictable Reactions:** Honking aggressively at NPCs at traffic stops can trigger wildly varying outcomes. Elderly citizens might panic and speed away, while aggressive pickup truck drivers may pull shotguns or baseball bats from their cabs.
* **Smartphone Culture:** If Lucia or Jason commit reckless acts or get into street fights on Ocean Drive, surrounding tourists pull out smartphones, live-stream the event, or call 911 in real time.
