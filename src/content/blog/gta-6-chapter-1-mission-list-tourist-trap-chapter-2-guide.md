The introductory chapter of ***Grand Theft Auto VI***, titled **"Just Like Paradise"**, immerses players into the humid, sun-bleached swamps of Mariana County and the Leonida Keys. 

Following Jason Duval’s small-town hustles for landlord Brian Heder (Stephen Root) through Lucia Caminos’ emotional release from the Leonida Penitentiary, Chapter 1 sets the narrative stakes for the entire game.

Here is the complete **22-mission list for Chapter 1**, the warning regarding the **"Tourist Trap" Point of No Return**, and how the transition into **Chapter 2 ("Paradise Lost")** works.

## 2. The Final Mission: "Tourist Trap" & Point of No Return

```
│               POINT OF NO RETURN WARNING                    │

│ Free-Roam Lockout   │ Yes (Temporarily disables open world) │
│ Safehouse Status    │ Key Lento locked until Chapter 4      │
│ Cash Preservation   │ Pocket cash locked into bail fund     │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The 22nd mission, **"Tourist Trap"**, serves as the dramatic narrative climax of Chapter 1:

* **The Setup:** After accepting Mattia's contract, an armed cartel ambush turns a simple marina score into a multi-agency state trooper pursuit across the Overseas Highway bridges.
* **The Lockout:** When the mission marker for *Tourist Trap* appears on your map, the game prompts an on-screen warning: *"Proceeding will advance the story and temporarily lock local regional activities."*
* **What Happens to Your Gear:** Your weapons, upgraded cars, and inventory are safely stored in your mobile trunk stash, but access to Jason's Key Lento home is temporarily restricted as the law forces the duo on the run.

## 3. What Happens in Chapter 2 ("Paradise Lost")?

<figure>
  <img src="/images/gta6-motel.webp" alt="Port Gellhorn Motel Safehouse in Chapter 2 GTA 6" width="960" height="540" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Completing *Tourist Trap* rolls the Chapter 2 title card **"Paradise Lost"**, moving the story north into **Port Gellhorn**:

* **New Safehouse:** Jason and Lucia set up in a grimy industrial motel overlooking the Port Gellhorn shipping canal.
* **Heist Targets:** You unlock large-scale industrial container heists, corrupt maritime brokers, safe-cracking jobs (Brooks Boatworks and Kingpin Bowling), and the critical moral choice to spare or execute foreman Arnold.
* **Duration:** Chapter 2 features 19 story missions spanning roughly 14 hours of campaign gameplay.
