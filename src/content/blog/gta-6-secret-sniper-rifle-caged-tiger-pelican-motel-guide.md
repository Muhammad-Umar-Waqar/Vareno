### How to Retrieve the Rifle:
1. Sneak onto the second-floor exterior balcony using the wooden service ladder on the motel's east wing.
2. Breach the warped door of **Room 4**. Subdue the lone scavenger guarding the interior.
3. The Laird 350 is leaning against a military cot next to two boxes of armor-piercing .338 rounds and an open gun-cleaning kit.
4. Equipping the rifle permanently unlocks it in your safehouse weapons locker and your vehicle's trunk stash.

## 3. The Caged Tiger Easter Egg: What Happens If You Open It?

Rockstar Games is renowned for satirizing bizarre Florida headlines, and the private exotic big-cat trade is front and center at this compound:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 CAGED TIGER INTERACTION CHOICES             │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Calm Choice         │ Drop raw venison / meat from inventory│
│ Aggressive Release  │ Shoot the padlock from safe distance  │
│ Tactical Ambush     │ Lure chasing police into the courtyard│
│ Environmental Fate  │ Tiger bounds into wetlands (despawns) │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **The Encounter:** In the center of the cracked swimming pool courtyard, surrounded by rusted barbed wire, sits a massive iron traveling circus cage housing a pacing adult Bengal tiger.
* **Shooting the Padlock:** Using a silenced pistol or sniper rifle to shoot off the padlock releases the beast. 
* **Tactical Weapon:** If you have active police heat or local gang enforcers tailing you into the motel lot, releasing the tiger causes it to immediately pounce on the nearest hostile NPC with devastating biomechanical takedown animations.
* **Feeding the Tiger:** Tossing raw meat (scavenged from local hunting or butcher shops) into the cage calms the tiger and prevents it from turning hostile toward Jason or Lucia.

## 4. Manager's Office Loot & Safe Code

Before leaving the compound, head into the reception office near the highway entrance:
* **The Safe:** Underneath the rotting linoleum floor behind the reception desk is a hidden steel floor safe.
* **The Combination:** The safe combination (**44-12-89**) is written on a yellowed motel receipt pinned to the corkboard.
* **The Payout:** Cracking the safe awards **$1,250 in unmarked bills** and an engraved vintage double-action six-shooter revolver.
