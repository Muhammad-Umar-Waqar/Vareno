## 1. Should You Spare Arnold in GTA 6?

During the Chapter 2 dock heist in Port Gellhorn, Lucia and Jason confront **Arnold**, a logistics manager caught double-crossing the crew with rival cartel smugglers. At the climax of the confrontation, players are presented with a definitive moral choice: **Spare Arnold or Eliminate Him**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      SPARING VS KILLING ARNOLD                         │
├─────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┬───────────────────────┤
│ Choice Outcome      │ Spare Arnold (Recommended)│ Eliminate Arnold     │
├─────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┼───────────────────────┤
│ Immediate Reward    │ $1,200 silence fee       │ $4,500 cash + weapon  │
│ Future Intel Drops  │ Unlocks 4 cargo tip-offs │ None (Locked out)     │
│ Moral / Trust Meter │ +15 Partner Trust boost  │ -10 Partner Trust     │
│ Police Heat Impact  │ Zero police suspicion    │ Moderate dock heat    │
└─────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┴───────────────────────┘
```

### The Verdict: Spare Him
We strongly recommend **sparing Arnold**. Sparing him keeps communication channels open. Roughly 8 in-game hours later, Arnold sends text messages revealing high-tier armored truck routes and cargo crate shipments across the Leonida highway network, netting players over **$45,000 in passive heist rewards**. Killing him provides instant gratification but deprives you of long-term income and partner trust.

## 2. How to "Fix Railing" in GTA 6 (Safehouse Objective)

One of the most viral and confusing early objectives occurs during the Vice City beach motel sequence, where players receive the task to **"Fix Railing"** on the upper balcony to obscure the police view of their getaway vehicle:

1. **Locate the Toolkit:** Enter the motel unit and walk into the bathroom. The red maintenance toolkit rests beneath the sink. Press the interact button to pick it up.
2. **Approach the Balcony Railing:** Walk out onto the exterior wooden balcony toward the highlighted yellow waypoint where the wooden balustrade has rotted or fallen away.
3. **Trigger the Interaction:** Stand directly in front of the broken section and hold the **Interact prompt** (`Right D-Pad` on PlayStation / Xbox).
4. **Complete the Stick Alignment:** Gently rotate the Right Analog Stick until the controller haptics vibrate steadily, then press `R2 / RT` to fasten the wooden brace.

> [!TIP]
> **Bug Workaround:** If the "Fix Railing" interaction prompt fails to appear, your character may be carrying an active weapon. Open your weapon wheel, select **Unarmed**, take three steps backward into the room, and re-approach the ledge.

## 3. Does GTA 6 Auto-Save? (And How Manual Saves Work)

Because of Leonida's aggressive law enforcement response and dynamic world events, knowing how and when the game records progress is crucial:

* **When GTA 6 Auto-Saves:** The game writes an auto-save checkpoint immediately after finishing story missions, discovering new map sectors, modifying cars in Los Santos/Vice City customs, purchasing real estate, or sleeping in a bed.
* **When Auto-Save is BLOCKED:** Auto-save is paused whenever:
  1. You have active **Wanted Stars** (Police pursuit active).
  2. You are engaged in open combat or weapon discharge.
  3. You are mid-mission during an active cutscene or driving sequence.

### How to Manually Save in GTA 6:
1. Open your protagonist's in-game smartphone (`Up D-Pad`).
2. Navigate to the **"Cloud Vault / Quick Save"** icon.
3. Select an empty save slot to lock in your exact progress and inventory loadout.

## 4. How Long is the GTA 6 Story Campaign?

*Grand Theft Auto VI* features the longest and most dense narrative campaign in Rockstar Games history:

* **Main Story Campaign:** **65 to 75 hours** (focusing strictly on primary heist arcs, Lucia and Jason character quests, and mandatory story missions).
* **Story + Side Content:** **90 to 105 hours** (incorporating stranger events, bounty hunting, Leonida Keys boat races, and nightlife empire investments).
* **100% Completionist Run:** **120 to 150+ hours** (discovering all Easter eggs, Everglades mythical creatures, full weapon arsenals, and property acquisitions).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you replay missions in GTA 6?
Yes. Just like *GTA 5* and *Red Dead Redemption 2*, you can pause the game, navigate to the **Game > Missions** menu, and replay any completed heist or story chapter to achieve gold medal objectives.

### What happens if your partner dies in free-roam?
If Lucia or Jason is critically wounded in free-roam, they will be transported to the nearest hospital in Vice City or Port Gellhorn. You will receive an in-game phone notification to pick them up, incurring a modest medical bill.
