### 1. Safe-Cracking by Touch
During robbery missions—such as cracking the Brooks Boatworks safe or the Delights manager vault with Vicki—you do not rely on visual HUD indicators. Slowly turning the **Left Stick** transmits minute mechanical tumbler clicks into your palm. When you hit the correct number, the **Right Trigger (R2)** locks solid, telling you to press it to open the lock.

### 2. Adaptive Trigger Weapon Mechanics
Every ballistic firearm has a custom force-feedback profile:
* **Two-Stage Triggers:** Lightly squeezing R2 readies a hip-fire stance; pulling past the resistance point fires the weapon.
* **Weapon Jams:** If a cheap street pistol jams due to swamp dirt, the R2 trigger physically locks halfway down until you tap **Square / X** to clear the chamber.
* **Heavy Caliber Recoil:** Firing the snub-nosed .44 Magnum produces a sharp, kick-back pulse that forces your finger upward.

### 3. Dynamic Police Siren Lightbar
When your wanted level increases to 3 stars or higher, the DualSense lightbar pulses with rapid **alternating Red and Blue strobes**, bathing dimly lit gaming rooms in realistic police cruiser lighting.
