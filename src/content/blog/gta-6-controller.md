Rockstar has not published a button guide for GTA 6. What is in writing today is the PlayStation store listing and the platforms on Rockstar's site.

The [US PlayStation listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/EP1004-PPSA01547_00-GTAVISTANDARD001/) says vibration and trigger effects are supported. That is DualSense, the pad that ships with PlayStation 5. The same listing marks the game PS5 Pro Enhanced, says it is 1 player, and says offline play is enabled. It also lists Remote Play.

## Xbox

[Rockstar's GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) lists Xbox Series X|S next to PlayStation 5 for November 19, 2026. The studio has not posted a separate feature list for the Xbox Wireless Controller. Series X|S players use that console's own controller unless Rockstar says otherwise later.

## What this does not settle

A supported trigger effect is not a control scheme. Rockstar has not said how driving, aiming, or the weapon wheel will feel, and this page will not invent a layout from trailer footage.

The same PlayStation lines are useful for a different question. "1 player" and "offline play enabled" match Rockstar's description of GTA VI as a single-player story. Whether a new online mode ships later is covered on the [platforms page](/platforms), which also holds the PC answer: no PC date is on the studio's site.
