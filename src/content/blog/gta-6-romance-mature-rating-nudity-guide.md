## 1. Official Age Ratings: Is GTA 6 18+ or 21+?

One of the most frequently asked questions is whether *Grand Theft Auto VI* carries an 18+ or 21+ classification. In the video game industry, **no global rating board issues a 21+ label**. Age ratings cap out at 18:

* **ESRB Rating (North America):** **M for Mature 17+** (Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Themes, Use of Drugs and Alcohol).
* **PEGI Rating (Europe & UK):** **PEGI 18** (Extreme Violence, Bad Language, Gambling Themes, In-Game Purchases).
* **USK (Germany):** **USK 18** (Uncut adult release).
* **ACB (Australia):** **R18+** (High-impact violence, adult themes, and drug references).

### Why Do People Think It's 21+?
Because the legal drinking and clubbing age in the United States (where the fictional state of Leonida / Florida is set) is 21, many in-game venues feature 21+ ID check parody signs. However, the legal purchase age for the game itself is 17 (US) or 18 (UK/Europe).

### Is GTA 6 Safe for Kids?
**No. GTA 6 is entirely inappropriate for children.** Parents should note that the game contains:
1. Graphic firearms combat and realistic dismemberment.
2. Uncensored profanity and sexual dialogue.
3. Interactive adult nightlife venues and illicit substance trade.
4. Moral ambiguity where protagonists commit felonies, armed robberies, and carjackings.

### Can You Kiss a Girl in GTA 6?
**Yes, in the context of Lucia and Jason's story.** Narrative story cutscenes, safehouse moments, and heist victory sequences feature intimate embraces, kisses, and affectionate dialogue between the two lovers. Trailer 1 showcased their intimate motel room conversation:
> *"The only way we’re gonna get through this is by sticking together, being a team. Trust?"*

**Can you kiss random NPCs?** No. Rockstar Games has designed the romance system around narrative emotional depth rather than arbitrary dating minigames. You cannot flirt with or kiss random pedestrians on the street.

## 3. Where is the Strip Club in GTA 6?

Vice City's nightlife is an iconic element of the franchise. Following in the footsteps of the *Vanilla Unicorn* in Los Santos, GTA 6 features expanded, hyper-realistic adult entertainment clubs:

* **Location:** The premier venue, colloquially named **The Vice Club**, is located in central Downtown Vice City, just a three-block drive west of Ocean Drive.
