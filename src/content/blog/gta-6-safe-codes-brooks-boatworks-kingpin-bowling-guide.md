During your criminal exploits across Port Gellhorn and Mariana County in ***Grand Theft Auto VI***, several high-value robbery targets feature fortified office safes containing thousands of dollars in illicit cash, bearer bonds, and rare equipment.

Two of the most heavily searched early-to-mid-game safes are the **Brooks Boatworks safe** and the **Kingpin Bowling safe**. 

Here are the verified safe combinations, room locations, alternative breach tactics, and loot breakdowns.

---

## 1. Brooks Boatworks Safe Combination & Location

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 BROOKS BOATWORKS SAFE DOSSIER               │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Target Location     │ Port Gellhorn Boat Basin & Marina     │
│ Safe Location       │ 2nd-Floor Mezzanine Supervisor Office │
│ Verified Safe Code  │ 6 8 2 3                               │
│ Concealment         │ Behind vintage nautical wall map      │
│ Heist Take          │ $12,500 Cash + Bearer Bonds           │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### How to Find and Unlock the Safe:
1. **Infiltrate Brooks Boatworks:** Located along the Port Gellhorn industrial canal waterfront. Sneak through the drydock hangar or eliminate the private shipyard guards.
2. **Head to the Mezzanine Office:** Climb the metal stairs at the back of the repair bay up to the supervisor's wood-paneled office.
3. **Reveal the Safe:** Interact with the large framed Florida coastline nautical chart hanging behind the mahogany desk. Jason or Lucia will swing the frame open to reveal the in-wall dial safe.
4. **Enter the Code `6823`:** Dial the safe combination **6823** using your controller's directional pad or left stick.
5. **Collect Loot:** You will recover **$12,500 in unmarked bank notes**, commercial shipping manifests (valuable for Arnold's side missions), and custom boat engine blueprints.

## 2. Kingpin Bowling Safe Combination & Location

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 KINGPIN BOWLING SAFE DOSSIER                │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Target Location     │ Kingpin Bowling Lanes, Port Gellhorn  │
│ Safe Location       │ Northeast Manager's Office (Backroom) │
│ Verified Safe Code  │ 0 3 0 0                               │
│ Alternative Breach  │ Sticky Bomb / Remote Explosive charge │
│ Heist Take          │ $8,400 Cash + Gold Watch Collection   │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### How to Open the Safe:
1. **Enter Kingpin Bowling:** Navigate to the Port Gellhorn entertainment plaza. Head past the bowling alleys into the restricted "Staff Only" corridor near lane 12.
2. **Locate the Northeast Office:** Pick the door lock with a lockpick or kick it open. The floor safe is positioned beneath the manager's desk.
3. **Enter the Code `0300`:** Dial **0300** to open the mechanism quietly without triggering silent alarms.
4. **The Sticky Bomb Alternative:** If you are in the middle of a police pursuit or don't want to spin the dial, plant a **Sticky Bomb** directly onto the safe hinge, step back 5 meters, and detonate. The door will blow off, allowing you to grab the cash and run.

## 3. General Safe-Cracking Tips in GTA 6

* **Listen for Audio Clicks:** If you encounter unmarked safes across Leonida without a code, slowly rotate the dial. Listen through your headset for a distinct heavy metal tumbler click, accompanied by a subtle controller rumble on the DualSense haptic triggers.
* **Inspect Corkboards & Burner Phones:** Rockstar always places environmental clues nearby. Check corkboards in breakrooms, calendar dates circled in red, or use the WAINK app to search nearby smartphone text threads for passwords.
