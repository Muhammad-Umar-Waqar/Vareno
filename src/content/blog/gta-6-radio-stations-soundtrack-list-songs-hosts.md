Rockstar Games invested heavily in securing premier music licensing for GTA 6. Key songs featured across the stations include:

## 3. Revolutionary Dynamic Audio & Spatial Physics

In *GTA 6*, radio audio isn't just a static audio stream played inside vehicles—it interacts physically with the 3D environment:

1. **Convertible & Window Physics:** If you roll down the windows or drive a convertible, bass frequencies reverberate off nearby concrete skyscrapers and tunnel walls.
2. **Drive-By Doppler Effect:** When another vehicle speeds past you blasting music, you hear realistic Doppler pitch shifts and muffled trunk bass.
3. **Club Exterior Bleed:** Walking past nightclub doors or beachfront bars in Vice City allows players to hear muffled kick drums vibrating through the glass before entering.

## 4. In-Game Smartphone Streaming App

For the first time in single-player franchise history, players are not tethered strictly to automobile radios. Using their in-game smartphone (`Up D-Pad`), Lucia and Jason can access an **audio streaming app**:

* **Headphone Walking Mode:** Listen to your favorite stations or custom mixtapes while jogging along the Vice City boardwalk or exploring the Leonida swamps.
* **Track Bookmarking:** "Shazam" any song playing in a store, restaurant, or vehicle by pressing the audio bookmark button, adding it to your saved in-game playlist.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can streamer mode disable copyrighted music in GTA 6?
Yes. Rockstar Games includes a built-in **"Streamer-Friendly Audio"** toggle in the audio settings menu, replacing licensed commercial tracks with original in-house instrumental compositions to prevent Twitch and YouTube copyright strikes.

### Are there classical or jazz stations in GTA 6?
Yes. Leonida features a dedicated classical music station broadcasting orchestral masterworks, perfect for satirical juxtaposition during high-speed bank heist escapes.
