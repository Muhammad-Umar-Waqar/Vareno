### How to Investigate the Bigfoot Easter Egg:
1. **Travel to Mount Caga:** Head to the northwestern elevated forest ridge overlooking the Ambrosia wetlands.
2. **Wait for Heavy Fog or Rain:** Use your safehouse bed to advance the clock until foggy atmospheric weather triggers between **2:00 AM and 4:00 AM**.
3. **Equip the Thermal Camera:** Equip your smartphone’s high-tech camera equipped with thermal/night vision. Listen closely for deep vocal howls echoing across the pine valleys.
4. **The Hunter's Cabin:** Near the river bend, you will discover an abandoned wooden shack containing newspaper clippings documenting *"The Leonida Skunk Ape"* alongside physical size 22 footprints leading into the swamp.

## 3. How to Clear "Unwanted Attention" (Pre-Wanted Stealth)

In *GTA 6*, the wanted system is vastly more realistic than prior entries. Before police trigger full sirens and shoot-on-sight protocols, characters enter the **"Unwanted Attention"** state:

* **What Causes Unwanted Attention:** Driving on sidewalks, speeding past police cruisers, carrying unsheathed weapons in public, or walking too close to high-security financial districts.
* **The Suspicion Radar:** Your mini-map pulses with a subtle yellow outline, indicating that bystanders or patrol officers are monitoring you.

### 4 Ways to Break Unwanted Attention Quickly:
1. **Duck Down in Your Vehicle:** Press the duck button (`X` on PlayStation / `A` on Xbox) to lower your head below the steering wheel, breaking the officer's line-of-sight.
2. **Follow Traffic Rules:** Slow down, stay in your lane, and do not make sharp turns until the yellow pulse clears.
3. **Change Your Clothing:** Enter any clothing store, laundromat, or your vehicle trunk to swap jackets and hats, neutralizing CCTV camera recognition.
4. **Switch Stolen Vehicles:** If an NPC dialed 911 with your car's make and model, abandoning the vehicle in an alleyway immediately ends police tracking.

## 4. The Law Enforcement "Criminal Profile" Database

One of the standout technological innovations in GTA 6 is the police department's dynamic criminal intelligence database:

* **Facial Recognition & CCTV:** Security cameras at banks, luxury retail stores, and highway toll booths actively scan Lucia and Jason. If you commit multiple crimes in the same county without wearing masks, your **Criminal Profile** rank rises.
* **County-Specific Bounties:** Warrants do not magically vanish when you cross county borders. A high criminal profile in Vice City Metro will cause local patrol units to stop and identify you, while rural sheriff departments in the Keys may have slower response times.
