### 1. PlayStation 5 Pro (The Definitive Console Experience)
* **Visual Presentation:** Utilizing Sony's proprietary **PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR)** AI upscaler, the PS5 Pro produces razor-sharp image clarity on 4K displays.
* **Ray Tracing Enhancements:** Global illumination bounce lighting and nocturnal neon reflections in Vice Beach puddle water feature double the ray-sample count of base consoles, virtually eliminating visual noise.
* **Frame Delivery:** Flawless 33.3ms frame times across intense police pursuits and chaotic weather storms.

### 2. Base PlayStation 5 & Xbox Series X (Parity Excellence)
* Both flagship base consoles deliver an exceptionally polished experience, dynamically scaling internal resolution between 1440p and reconstructive 4K.
* Rockstar’s proprietary temporal anti-aliasing (TAA) keeps fine details—such as palm fronds, chainlink fences, and ocean ripples—clean and flicker-free.
* The 30 FPS cap is virtually locked, with unnoticeable single-frame drops occurring only during massive chain-reaction explosions.

### 3. Xbox Series S ("The Quaint Little Beast")
* While social media skeptics feared the Series S would fail to run *GTA 6*, Rockstar’s optimization engineers pulled off a minor miracle.
* Running at a dynamic 900p to 1080p with dialed-back crowd density and simplified screen-space reflections, the Series S maintains a solid 30 FPS across 90% of gameplay.
* Minor frame dips to **26–28 FPS** occur primarily during dense marshland navigation in Mariana County or intense 6-star multi-cruiser shootouts.
