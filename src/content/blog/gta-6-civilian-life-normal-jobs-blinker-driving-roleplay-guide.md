## 2. How to Use Turn Signals & Blinkers

A feature that astonished early players is Rockstar’s inclusion of functional vehicle turn signals:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 HOW BLINKERS WORK IN GTA 6                  │
├──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Manual Control       │ Tap D-Pad Left / Right while driving │
│ Automatic Mode       │ Settings > Gameplay > Smart Blinkers │
│ Dashboard Indicator  │ Active green blinker arrows in cabin │
│ NPC Reaction         │ AI drivers yield when signals blink  │
└──────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Manual Signaling:** Tapping **Left or Right on the D-Pad** while inside any street-legal vehicle activates the corresponding exterior turn signal.
* **First-Person Dashboard Feedback:** In first-person mode, the instrument cluster displays an illuminated green ticking indicator light with genuine relay clicking audio.
* **Smart GPS Blinkers:** When enabled in the gameplay settings, the vehicle automatically engages the turn signal 50 meters before an upcoming GPS waypoint turn. Adjacent AI traffic actively slows down to let you merge into their lane.

### 1. Vice City Cab Driver
By purchasing or renting a taxi medallion through the municipal office in Vice City, Jason or Lucia can pick up passengers across Ocean Drive, Vice Shores, and Little Haiti. Fares increase dynamically based on smooth driving, adhering to speed limits, and avoiding sudden braking.

### 2. Leonida Parcel Courier
Operating from Port Gellhorn’s industrial shipping hubs, players can load delivery vans with packages and navigate suburban neighborhoods, delivering parcels to doorsteps while managing delivery time windows.

### 3. Everglades Eco-Tour Captain
Pilot airboats and catamaran charters through Mariana County, pointing out alligators, flamingo flocks, and swamp flora to enthusiastic tourists. Avoiding wildlife strikes yields generous civilian tips.

### 4. Licensed Commercial Fishing
Purchase deep-sea rods and trawling permits at the Key Lento bait shop. Catching tarpon, snapper, and mahi-mahi and selling them directly to local marina fish markets provides clean, legitimate profit with zero criminal record.

* **Home Improvement:** As seen in Jason’s Key Lento safehouse, players can complete small DIY repairs—fixing rotten porch boards, clearing overgrown yard vines, and repairing Mandy’s plumbing—to raise safehouse comfort ratings.
* **Gym Conditioning:** Visiting athletic facilities in Vice Beach allows players to jog on treadmills, lift weights, and spar in boxing rings, providing passive stamina and carry-weight bonuses.
* **Nutrition & Grocery Shopping:** Instead of eating solely to regenerate combat health, dining at Vice City diners or buying fresh produce at roadside markets maintains player energy levels and stamina recovery.
