Rockstar has not published a start date for Grand Theft Auto VI. The earliest film on the studio's own trailer page is Trailer 1, dated December 4, 2023. Launch on that same site is November 19, 2026. From the public trailer to launch is a little under three years. Anything before Trailer 1 is not a date this guide will treat as official.

## What Rockstar has put on the record

The [trailer page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer) lists four main films:

1. Trailer 1, December 4, 2023
2. Trailer 2, May 6, 2025
3. An Extended Look, August 27, 2026
4. Launch Trailer, October 15, 2026

The [GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) now says the game is coming November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That page is the date to trust when older posts disagree with it.

## Why a year-count goes wrong

Fans have guessed at 2014, 2018, or the years after Red Dead Redemption 2. Those guesses describe how long people have been waiting. They are not a Rockstar production calendar. Until the studio prints a start date, the honest answer is the public one: announced on screen in December 2023, scheduled now for November 19, 2026.

The schedule itself moved. A 2025 window gave way to May 26, 2026, and then to November 19, 2026. The short version of that story lives on the [GTA 6 release date](/release-date) page. The longer pass is in [how the date moved twice](/blog/gta-6-delay-history).
