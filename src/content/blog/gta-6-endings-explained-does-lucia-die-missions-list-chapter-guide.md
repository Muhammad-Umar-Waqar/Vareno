## 2. All 5 Story Chapters & 91 Missions Breakdown

The narrative arc follows Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval through a five-act crime saga spanning every corner of the state of Leonida:

### Chapter 1: "Just Like Paradise" (Missions 1 – 18)
* **Setting:** Mariana County, Key Lento, and the Florida Everglades.
* **Plot:** Lucia is released on parole from the Leonida Penitentiary. She reunites with Jason in Key Lento, working for smuggler Brian Heder (Stephen Root) in low-stakes robberies, safehouse renovations, and debt collections (including the viral "Pipework" mission).
* **Key Missions:** *Parole Hearing*, *Lab Fees*, *Pipework*, *Bait & Tackle*, *The First Score*.

### Chapter 2: "Paradise Lost" (Missions 19 – 37)
* **Setting:** Port Gellhorn and industrial shipping yards.
* **Plot:** Escaping small-town heat, the duo takes on high-stakes heists against Port Gellhorn container yards. Players make crucial moral decisions, including whether to spare or execute yard foreman Arnold.
* **Key Missions:** *Container Row*, *Unwanted Attention*, *The Foreman’s Choice*, *High Water Mark*.

### Chapter 3: "Tits, Ass, Drug Money" (Missions 38 – 59)
* **Setting:** Vice City downtown, Little Haiti, and Starfish Island.
* **Plot:** Jason and Lucia arrive in Vice City, penetrating the illicit financial networks of modern cartels, high-end nightclubs, and crypto money laundering rings.
* **Key Missions:** *Vice Lights*, *Ocean Drive Sweep*, *Digital Cleanse*, *The Marina Drop*.

### Chapter 4: "Vice City, Baby" (Missions 60 – 78)
* **Setting:** Metropolitan Vice City, airport runways, and luxury beachfront penthouses.
* **Plot:** The couple plans and executes multi-phase heists against major commercial banks and corrupt state politicians while federal task forces tighten their dragnet.
* **Key Missions:** *Federal Wire*, *Black Bag Operation*, *Asset Forfeiture*, *The Grand Reserve*.

### Chapter 5: "Top of the World, Ma" — The Finale (Missions 79 – 91)
* **Setting:** Offshore islands, state capitol grounds, and luxury yacht showdowns.
* **Plot:** The endgame forces Jason and Lucia to confront law enforcement, cartel syndicates, and their own trust in each other.
* **Key Missions:** *Point of No Return*, *Trust Fall*, *Checkmate*, *Sunset Over Leonida*.

The fate of Lucia Caminos is not predetermined—it is determined by the cumulative choices made throughout Chapters 1 through 5:

### Ending 1: "Bonnie & Clyde" (The Best / Golden Ending)
* **Condition:** High Relationship Trust (above 80%), balanced heist revenue in the Family Savings Account, and choosing cooperative options during Chapter 5.
* **Outcome:** Jason and Lucia execute their final offshore escape together. Both protagonists survive with tens of millions of dollars, retiring to an unlisted private villa outside Leonida. Free-roam mode unlocks both characters permanently with complete access to all properties and vehicles.

### Ending 2: "Lucia's Sacrifice" (Tragic Ending A)
* **Condition:** Choosing self-preservation options as Jason, failing to support Lucia during the Federal ambush in Mission 88, or allowing the trust meter to drop to zero.
* **Outcome:** Lucia provides covering fire to allow Jason and their crew to escape the marina docks, succumbing to tactical wounds. **Lucia dies.** In post-game free-roam, players control Jason alone, accompanied by somber narrative voicemail logs and unique dialogue reflecting his grief.

### Ending 3: "Jason's Last Stand" (Tragic Ending B)
* **Condition:** Choosing reckless, aggressive aggression as Jason while Lucia prioritizes financial caution and clean getaways.
* **Outcome:** Jason sacrifices himself during an armored roadblock breach to ensure Lucia evades the VCPD federal dragnet. **Jason dies, and Lucia survives.** Post-game free-roam is played exclusively as Lucia, who inherits all safehouses and syndicate assets.

### Ending 4: "Down in Flames" (The Worst Ending)
* **Condition:** Minimal relationship trust, zero family savings, and choosing betrayal options during the final confrontation.
* **Outcome:** Both characters turn on each other amidst a massive joint-strike raid by state troopers and cartel enforcers. **Both Lucia and Jason die.**

## 4. How to Ensure Lucia and Jason Both Survive

To unlock the canonical Golden Ending where both characters survive, follow these rules:

1. **Keep the Family Savings Account Funded:** After every heist, deposit at least 40% of the take into the joint safehouse vault rather than spending it all on luxury cars or individual weapons.
2. **Back Up Your Partner in Combat:** During dual-protagonist missions, switch quickly to suppress enemies flanking your partner.
3. **Choose Loyalty Dialogue Prompts:** In cutscenes with NPCs like Mandy or criminal contacts, consistently choose options that reaffirm trust and partnership.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you still play the game after beating the story?
Yes. Kotaku and IGN confirmed that regardless of which ending you trigger, *GTA 6* features extensive post-credits free-roam gameplay. You can complete all remaining side quests, street races, compendium wildlife sightings, vehicle chop-shop jobs, and underwater treasure hunts.

### Can you replay missions in GTA 6 to get different endings?
No. Unlike *GTA 5*, *Grand Theft Auto VI* does not feature an instant mission replay menu from the pause screen. Rockstar designed story consequences to be permanent. Players are strongly advised to keep a **manual save file** at the start of Chapter 5.
