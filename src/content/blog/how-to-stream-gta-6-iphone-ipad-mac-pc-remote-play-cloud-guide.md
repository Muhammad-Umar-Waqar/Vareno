## 1. Streaming GTA 6 on iPhone, iPad & Mac (PS Remote Play)

```
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Required Host       │ PlayStation 5 Console (Powered or Rest│

└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step-by-Step Setup:
1. **Enable Remote Play on PS5:** On your PS5 console, navigate to **Settings > System > Remote Play** and toggle **Enable Remote Play** to 'ON'.
2. **Configure Rest Mode:** Go to **Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode** and enable **Stay Connected to the Internet** and **Enable Turning on PS5 from Network**.
3. **Install the App:** Download the official **PS Remote Play** app from the Apple App Store on your iPhone, iPad, or macOS App Store.
4. **Pair Your DualSense Controller:** 
   * On your DualSense controller, hold the **Create Button** and the central **PS Button** together for 3 seconds until the light bar flashes blue.
   * Open your iPhone/iPad/Mac **Bluetooth Settings** and select **"DualSense Wireless Controller"**.
5. **Connect & Launch:** Sign in with your PlayStation Network credentials in the app and tap your PS5 console to boot up *GTA 6* instantly.

## 3. How to Eliminate Input Lag & Stutter

Streaming an intensive open-world action title requires precise frame pacing, especially during high-speed police pursuits:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 LATENCY REDUCTION CHECKLIST                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Hardwire your console to your router via Ethernet.       │
│ 2. Use 5GHz Wi-Fi (avoid crowded 2.4GHz home bands).        │
│ 3. In PS Remote Play settings, set Video Quality to 1080p.  │
│ 4. Turn off HDR in remote app to prevent bandwidth choke.   │
│ 5. Close background torrents or 4K video streams on home net│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **The Ethernet Golden Rule:** Connecting your host console directly to your router with an Ethernet cable cuts latency by over **60%**, eliminating the micro-stutter that ruins vehicle cornering.
* **Disable HDR on Mobile:** Turning off HDR streaming within the Remote Play settings prevents stream compression artifacts and frees up 20% of network packet bandwidth.
