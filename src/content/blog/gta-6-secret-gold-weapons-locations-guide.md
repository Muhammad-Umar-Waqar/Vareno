## 2. Exact Locations & How to Unlock Each Weapon

### 1. Hawk & Little Gold-Plated Revolvers (Mariana Plantation Manor)
* **Location:** Northern Mariana County, inside the decaying 19th-century sugar cane estate.
* **How to Obtain:** Navigate to the master suite on the second floor. Move the framed landscape painting on the north wall to reveal an antique tumbler safe. Crack the safe using DualSense haptic clicks to retrieve the engraved matched pair.

### 2. The "Electric Fang" Tactical Stun Baton (Port Gellhorn Marina)
* **Location:** Berth 6 Security Office at the Port Gellhorn commercial docks.
* **How to Obtain:** Lockpick the yellow maintenance cage at night. This weapon delivers 50,000 volts of non-lethal electrical charge, allowing you to instantly subdue armored private security without generating murder heat.

### 3. Gold Cartel AK-47 Assault Rifle (Starfish Island Villa)
* **Location:** Basement vault beneath the Spanish Revival villa on western Starfish Island.
* **How to Obtain:** Infiltrate the estate during the Chapter 3 cartel reconnaissance mission. The solid gold-plated Kalashnikov sits on a velvet rack inside the underground counting room. It features a permanent 40-round drum magazine and zero recoil climb.

### 4. Snub-Nosed .44 Marsh Magnum (Sunken Airboat)
* **Location:** Mariana Marsh coordinates (24.891, -81.244).
* **How to Obtain:** Dive 10 feet underwater to inspect a rusted fan-boat wreckage. Cut open the steel tackle box using a crowbar to grab this close-range handcannon with massive point-blank stopping power.

### 5. Suppressed Tac-9 Machine Pistol (Key Lento Bait Shop)
* **Location:** Backroom office of Key Lento Bait & Tackle.
* **How to Obtain:** Inspect the rear wall behind the taxidermy marlin. Interacting with the loose drywall reveals a sound-suppressed Tac-9 with an integrated whisper can, perfect for stealth robbery runs.

### 6. Trench Sweeper 12-Gauge Shotgun (Mount Kalaga Railcar)
* **Location:** Overgrown train spur west of Mount Kalaga.
* **How to Obtain:** Pry open the rusted boxcar sliding door. This vintage combat shotgun fires heavy copper slug rounds that penetrate light vehicular armor.

### 7. 1920s Drum-Mag Tommy Gun (Prohibition Rum-Runner Wreck)
* **Location:** Shallow reef 500 meters south of Key Lento.
* **How to Obtain:** Free-dive to the barnacle-encrusted wooden schooner hull. Stored in a waterproof grease-lined leather case, this .45 ACP submachine gun fires at 800 rounds per minute with an authentic 50-round drum.

### 8. Tactical Bowie Hunting Knife (Everglades Gator Farm)
* **Location:** Butcher shed behind the roadside alligator attraction.
* **How to Obtain:** Embedded in a heavy wooden butcher block. The serrated carbon-steel blade executes silent, one-hit stealth takedowns from behind.

### 9. Heavy .50 Anti-Material Sniper Rifle (Naval Radar Gantry)
* **Location:** Restricted Leonida Naval Air Station.
* **How to Obtain:** Scale the exterior maintenance ladder to the catwalk of the active phased-array radar tower. This weapon can disable vehicle engines and police helicopter rotors with a single armor-piercing shot.
