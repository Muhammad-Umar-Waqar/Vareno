## 3. When Will GTA 6 Online Come Out?

Rockstar Games has maintained its gold-standard phased release model:

1. **Phase 1 (Now Live):** Focus 100% on the single-player campaign, world exploration, and technical stability on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
2. **Phase 2 (Projected Early 2027):** Launch the next-generation **GTA Online** ecosystem. 

This staggered approach mirrors *GTA V* (where GTA Online launched 14 days later in 2013) and *Red Dead Redemption 2* (where Red Dead Online launched one month after release in November 2018). For *GTA 6*, industry analysts project the standalone online service will roll out in **early 2027**, accompanied by dedicated character creation in modern Vice City.

## 4. The "Cyberleek" Xbox Build Facts

Following spikes in Google Trends for *"cyberleek"*, here is what actually transpired in the tech security space:

* **The Leak Claim:** A hacking group operating under the alias **Cyberleek** claimed to host leaked pre-release internal development packages sourced from Xbox telemetry files.
* **The Reality:** While data scrapers extracted 3D wireframe models and audio samples, the distribution packages **intentionally lacked the executable application file (`.exe` / `.xbe`)**, rendering the files entirely non-functional. Legitimate players have nothing to gain from downloading these dubious files, which often carry high malware risks.
