Here is the complete, official step-by-step guide on how to game share *GTA 6* on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, along with clarification on playing via older hardware and cloud remote play.

## 1. How to Game Share GTA 6 on PlayStation 5

Sony PlayStation officially supports family game sharing through the **Console Sharing and Offline Play** system:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 PS5 GAME SHARING ARCHITECTURE               │
├────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Account A (Buyer)      │ Purchases GTA 6 digitally on PSN   │
│ Console B (Friend)     │ Set as Primary ("Console Sharing") │
│ Console A (Buyer's PS5)│ Plays online on Buyer's account    │
│ Result                 │ Both consoles play simultaneously  │
└────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step-by-Step PS5 Setup:
1. **Log in on Friend's Console:** Sign into your PlayStation Network account (the account that purchased GTA 6) on your friend or family member's PS5 console.
2. **Access Settings:** Go to **Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Console Sharing and Offline Play**.
3. **Enable Sharing:** Select **Enable** (or verify that it is not disabled).
4. **Download the Game:** Navigate to the Game Library and queue the 150GB+ *Grand Theft Auto VI* download onto their internal NVMe SSD.
5. **Switch Back to Friend's Profile:** Once downloaded, your friend switches back to their own personal PSN user profile. They can now launch GTA 6, unlock their own trophies, and play online.
6. **Play on Your Own Console:** On your original PS5, log in with your primary account. As long as your console has an active internet connection to verify server licenses, you can play simultaneously with your friend.

## 2. How to Game Share GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S

Microsoft Xbox offers a similar seamless sharing system via the **Home Xbox** setting:

1. Sign into your Xbox account on your friend's Xbox Series X or Series S.
2. Press the Xbox button, go to **Profile & system > Settings > General > Personalization > My home Xbox**.
3. Select **Make this my home Xbox**.
4. Download *Grand Theft Auto VI* from the digital library.
5. Your friend can now log into their own Xbox gamertag and play GTA 6, while you continue enjoying the game on your secondary home console.

## 3. Can You Play GTA 6 on Xbox One or PS4?

One of the most persistent search queries is whether last-generation consoles (PS4, PS4 Pro, Xbox One, Xbox One X) can run GTA 6.

* **Native Release:** **100% Impossible.** GTA 6's volumetric lighting, dense crowd AI, and high-speed asset streaming mandate high-bandwidth NVMe SSDs and Zen 2 / RDNA 2 processor architectures that 8th-generation hardware cannot deliver.
* **The Remote Play Workaround:** If you own a PS5 or Xbox Series X placed in your living room, you can stream the gameplay directly to an older console, laptop, or mobile tablet using **PlayStation Remote Play** or **Xbox Remote Play** over high-speed local Wi-Fi.
