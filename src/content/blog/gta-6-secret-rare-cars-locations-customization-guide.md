### 1. Grotti Cheetah Spyder (Starfish Island Private Slip)
* **Location:** Behind a gated Mediterranean estate on northern Starfish Island.
* **How to Obtain:** Sneak past the automated security gate using a silenced weapon to disable the CCTV camera box. The keys are located on the patio bar table.

### 2. Declasse Tulip 454 Drag Edition (Mariana Swamp Barn Find)
* **Location:** Inside an overgrown corrugated iron tobacco shed in central Mariana County (coordinates 25.104, -80.892).
* **How to Obtain:** Hook the shed doors to Jason's pickup truck using a winch cable to tear the rusted doors open. Requires bringing a battery jumper kit to ignite the engine.

### 3. Pegassi Infernus EV Hypercar (Vice Shores Luxury Hotel)
* **Location:** Parked in the VIP valet circle in front of the Delano Grand Resort on Vice Beach between 10:00 PM and 2:00 AM in-game time.
* **How to Obtain:** Bribe the valet attendant for $500 or use an electronic signal cloner while standing within 5 meters of the vehicle owner.

### 4. Vapid Ripper 4x4 Heavy Trophy Truck (Mount Kalaga Ridge)
* **Location:** Parked near an abandoned radio relay hut overlooking the Mount Kalaga national forest.
* **How to Obtain:** Equipped with heavy off-road tires, long-travel suspension, and high-intensity roof spotlights, ideal for deep Everglades exploration.

### 5. Bravado Banshee GTS (Port Gellhorn Shipping Container)
* **Location:** Inside a locked navy-blue shipping container located at Berth 14 of the Port Gellhorn container terminal.
* **How to Obtain:** Use a handheld acetylene torch to cut the padlock during Chapter 2 or Chapter 3 free-roam.

## 2. How to Prevent Rare Cars from Despawning

In *GTA 6*, vehicle persistence is tied to ownership registration. If you steal a rare supercar off the boulevard and abandon it during a shootout, it will despawn unless properly registered:

│              VEHICLE RETENTION & TRACKING CHECKLIST         │
├───────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Legal Tracker  │ Install VIN tracker at Joey's Ganado Auto│
│ 2. Safehouse Bay  │ Park inside marked garage parking boxes │

│ 4. Impound Rescue │ Pay $1,200 fine if seized by State Patrol│

* **Install a Lo-Jack VIN Tracker:** Take any stolen exotic to **Joey's Ganado Auto Repairs** in Key Lento or **Vice City Custom Works**. Installing an encrypted tracker ($2,500) permanently links the vehicle to Jason and Lucia’s phone GPS app.
* **Use Safehouse Garage Bays:** Parking rare cars within designated yellow boundaries at safehouses (Jason's Key Lento yard or the Port Gellhorn motel slot) guarantees permanent saving through story chapter transitions.
* **Impound Lot Retrieval:** If you are busted or killed by police while driving your custom rare car, check the **Leonida Highway Patrol Impound Yard** in Downtown Vice City. You can legally reclaim it for $1,200 or hop the perimeter razor wire at night for a stealth jailbreak.
