When Jason and Lucia are forced to flee Key Lento following the dramatic climax of Mission 22 ("Tourist Trap"), they take refuge at the **Starlet Motel** on the outskirts of Port Gellhorn. 

Residing just down the balcony in **Room 10** is Vicki—a sharp-tongued, weathered entertainer working the night shift at **Delights**, a notorious roadside adult entertainment venue operated by local crime boss Frankie. 

Vicki’s insider knowledge of Frankie’s illicit safehouse cash makes her an invaluable strategic partner for Jason and Lucia's growing criminal ambitions.

## 2. Location: Finding Room 10 at the Starlet Motel

To locate Vicki outside of scripted mission markers:
1. Navigate to the **Starlet Motel** located along Highway 9 near the Port Gellhorn shipping canal.
2. Head up the rusted metal staircase to the second-floor balcony walkway.
3. Knock on the door of **Room 10** (distinguishable by string fairy lights and a neon flamingo in the window).
4. Between **8:00 PM and 2:00 AM**, Vicki will be working her shift at Delights on Eastern Boulevard. During daytime hours, she is resting in Room 10 and available for optional character dialogue.

## 3. Mission Walkthrough: "Afternoon Delight"

The centerpiece of Vicki’s storyline is the multi-stage robbery mission **"Afternoon Delight"**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│            "AFTERNOON DELIGHT" MISSION OBJECTIVES           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Meet Vicki at the Starlet Motel balcony to review intel. │
│ 2. Infiltrate Delights through the rear loading dock.       │
│ 3. Subdue Frankie's two private security bouncers quietly.  │
│ 4. Crack the VIP manager's safe using the audio stethoscope.│
│ 5. Evade Frankie's enforcers along the Port Gellhorn docks. │
│ 6. Split the $48,000 cash cut with Vicki back at Room 10.   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step-by-Step Strategy:
* **The Infiltration:** Vicki leaves the service alley door unlatched behind the club dumpster. Approach the alley on foot without drawing weapons to avoid alerting the parking lot valet.
* **Non-Lethal Takedown:** Sneak behind the security guard in the dressing corridor. Using a stealth chokehold preserves your civilian heat rating and prevents an immediate 3-star police response.
* **Safe-Cracking Mechanics:** Frankie's wall safe uses the new DualSense haptic feedback system. Slowly turn the safe dial until the controller vibrates intensely, indicating each tumbler lock clicks into place.
* **The Getaway:** Exiting Delights triggers an armed confrontation with Frankie's personal muscle. Have Lucia provide sniper overwatch from the roof while Jason maneuvers the getaway car into the canal service tunnel.
