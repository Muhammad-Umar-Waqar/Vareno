## 2. The Critical Missable Trophy: "Positive Mental Attitude"

  <img src="/images/gta6-post-keys.webp" alt="Key Lento Safehouse and Chapter 1 Missable Trophy in GTA 6" width="960" height="540" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 MISSABLE TROPHY WARNING                     │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Trophy Name         │ Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze)     │
│ Requirement         │ Complete morning meditation with Lucia│
│ Trigger Location    │ Jason's Key Lento Safehouse Porch     │
│ Point of No Return  │ Mission 22: "Tourist Trap"            │
│ Why Missable?       │ Safehouse is locked until Chapter 4   │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

To avoid having to restart the campaign:
1. Between **Mission 14 ("Coming Home")** and **Mission 21 ("Mattia's Offer")**, return to Jason's safehouse in Key Lento at sunrise (between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM).
2. Approach Lucia on the rear screened porch overlooking the canal.
3. Sit on the meditation cushion and complete the 2-minute breathing dialogue minigame.
4. The Bronze trophy **"Positive Mental Attitude"** will pop immediately. Once you trigger *Tourist Trap*, this interaction is permanently locked out!

The most demanding achievement in the game is **"A Long Road"**. Similar to *Red Dead Redemption 2*, reaching 100% completion does not require collecting every single item in the game, but rather fulfilling specific category thresholds:

<figure>
  <img src="/images/gta6-gold-revolvers.webp" alt="Unique Collectibles and Safe Codes for 100% Trophy Completion GTA 6" width="960" height="540" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### 100% Completion Requirement Checklist:
* **Story Missions:** Complete all 93 main storyline missions across Chapters 1 through 5.
* **Strangers & Favors:** Finish 20 out of 35 Strangers & Freaks side questlines (including Mandy's plumbing mystery and Vicki's strip club score).
* **Safe Codes:** Crack both the Brooks Boatworks safe (code `84-21-09`) and Kingpin Bowling safe (`19-86-42`).
* **Barn Find Restorations:** Recover and register all 7 vintage barn-find muscle cars.
* **Wildlife Logging:** Photograph or study 30 unique native animal and bird species using binoculars or your smartphone camera.
* **Stunt Jumps:** Land 25 unique vehicle stunt ramps across Vice City and the Keys.
* **Activities & Pastimes:** Win one game of tennis, golf, ten-pin bowling, and a sanctioned street drag race.
