* **The Physical Leonida Map Poster:** Unfolding the box reveals a high-gloss, full-color fold-out paper map. Side A displays the entire geography of Leonida—spanning the Overseas Highway, Key Lento, Lake Leonida, and Mount Kalaga. Side B provides a high-density tourist street grid of Vice City, including Ocean Drive, Starfish Island, and Vice Port.
* **Resale & Ownership:** The physical disc is playable fully offline for the single-player campaign once initial installation and authentication are completed, preserving true physical game ownership.
