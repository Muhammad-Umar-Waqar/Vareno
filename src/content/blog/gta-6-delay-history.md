GTA 6's public schedule has had two big moves. The December 2023 trailer was framed as a 2025 release. In May 2025 Rockstar set May 26, 2026. In November 2025 the studio moved the launch to November 19, 2026. That November date is the one on [Rockstar's GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) today.

The short answer to "when does it come out" stays on the [GTA 6 release date](/release-date) page. This note is the timeline behind it.

## December 4, 2023

[Trailer 1](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer) is dated December 4, 2023 on Rockstar's trailer page. It introduced Jason, Lucia, Vice City, and the state of Leonida, with a 2025 window. Rockstar did not ship the game in 2025.

## May 2025

Trailer 2 is dated May 6, 2025. Around that trailer, the launch was set for May 26, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That May 2026 date is the one a later announcement replaced.

## November 2025

Rockstar then moved Grand Theft Auto VI to Thursday, November 19, 2026. As of November 16, 2026, the studio's site still shows November 19. A further delay would have to appear there, or in a new Take-Two release, before this guide treats it as fact.

## What did not move in the June price announcement

On June 24, 2026, [Take-Two announced pre-orders](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi/) and repeated November 19, 2026. Standard is $79.99. Ultimate is $99.99. Those figures are on the [price page](/price).

Pre-load is the one date that reads differently across official pages. Take-Two's June release listed November 12, 2026. Rockstar's [November 10, 2026 pre-load page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/pre-load-now) says pre-loading is available for people who have pre-ordered. If you have a pre-order, check the console store rather than waiting on the older date.
