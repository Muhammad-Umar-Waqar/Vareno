Why did early reports quote **212 GB**?
When downloading a game on modern consoles, the operating system requires **temporary scratch space** to download compressed installation archives, unpack asset blocks, and apply patch deltas simultaneously. 
* While you need **at least 212 GB of free space** to initiate the download, the installer deletes temporary compressed staging files once complete.
* The final, permanent footprint on your internal SSD settles at **145.2 GB on PS5** and **154.0 GB on Xbox Series X**.
