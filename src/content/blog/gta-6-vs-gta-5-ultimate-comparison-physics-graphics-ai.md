## 2. Physics & Biomechanical Character Animations

In *GTA 5*, character movement was governed by a hybrid of traditional keyframe animations and NaturalMotion's Euphoria ragdoll engine. While groundbreaking in 2013, characters frequently clipped through car doors and rigid objects.

*GTA 6* introduces **Biomechanical Skeletal Adaptation**:
* **Door Squeezing:** When parking tightly against concrete pillars, Lucia or Jason dynamically turn their torso sideways to squeeze through tight gaps rather than phasing through sheet metal.
* **Carrying Momentum:** Characters carrying objects (such as toolboxes or six-packs of soda) will swim with the cargo under one arm and climb dock ladders one-handed without dropping the item.
* **Weight & Impact:** Melee punches deliver localized blunt trauma. When an opponent is knocked out, they do not despawn—they slowly recover, rub their bruised head, and stumble back to their car.

## 3. Water Dynamics & Maritime Simulation

Water in *GTA 5* was rendered as a flat animated plane with procedural wave shaders. In *GTA 6*, water is a fully simulated physical entity:
* **True Hydrodynamics:** Ocean swells and tidal currents exert genuine hydrodynamic drag on boats and swimmers. Navigating through choppy tropical storms creates realistic hull slamming and airborne spray.
* **Underwater Caustics:** Sunlight refracting through ocean waves projects shifting, real-time light ribbons onto coral reefs and sea beds.
* **Wetness Shaders:** Clothes and skin absorb water progressively; stepping into waist-deep swamps drenches fabrics realistically up to the exact water line.

## 4. Vehicle Dynamics & Soft-Body Deformation

While *GTA 5* received criticism for toning down *GTA IV*'s realistic vehicle deformation in favor of arcade-style high-speed responsiveness, *GTA 6* strikes the ultimate balance between control and physical realism:
* **Tire Grip & Weight Transfer:** Cars have tangible curb weight. Sudden high-speed cornering induces suspension roll, while accelerating on rain-slicked asphalt induces realistic hydroplaning.
* **Localized Deformation:** Rather than generic dent textures, vehicles experience localized crumple zones, shattered glass shards with realistic tinting, and physical trunk latches that can pop open during violent collisions.
