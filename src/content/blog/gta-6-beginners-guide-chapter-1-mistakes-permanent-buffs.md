### Mistake 1: Triggering the "Tourist Trap" Point of No Return
Mission 22, **"Tourist Trap"**, forces Jason and Lucia to flee Key Lento under high-intensity law enforcement pursuit. Once started, you cannot return to Jason’s coastal starter home or finish incomplete fishing permits until late in Chapter 4. Clear all Key Lento exploration before accepting this job!

### Mistake 2: Burning Early Cash on Vehicle Customization
Do not spend your starter cash on high-end rims, turbochargers, or luxury paint jobs in Key Lento. Early vehicles are frequently abandoned during police pursuits. Save your capital until Chapter 2, where you unlock Port Gellhorn chop shops and vehicle insurance tracking.

### Mistake 3: Stashing High-Tier Guns in Unlocked Trunks
The physical inventory in *GTA 6* allows you to store excess rifles and shotguns in your car trunk. However, leaving your car unlocked in public lots allows NPC scavengers to loot your trunk. **Always purchase the $45 Remote Key Fob at Ammu-Nation** to double-lock your vehicle.

### Mistake 4: Skipping Optional Relationship Gestures
Optional domestic tasks—such as repairing Jason's porch railing, bringing fresh coffee to Lucia, holding hands while walking through town, and sharing heist loot evenly—feed directly into your **Relationship Trust Meter**. Dropping below 2 stars creates hostility during co-op gunfights and permanently blocks the 4-Star Good Ending.

### Mistake 5: Neglecting Food & Physical Nutrition
In *GTA 6*, characters experience metabolic fatigue. Skipping meals for several in-game days drains maximum sprint stamina by 25% and causes weapon aim sway. Stop at roadside burger stands or keep beef jerky in your inventory to stay at peak combat performance.
