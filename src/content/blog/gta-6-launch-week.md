GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This is the short list for the week of November 16, with the longer answers linked underneath.

## Check the date once

The date to plan around is November 19, 2026. The US PlayStation listing shows 5:00 a.m. UTC. Other stores can unlock on a different local clock. The [release date page](/release-date) has the countdown and the delay history.

Rockstar's site, as of November 16, 2026, still shows November 19. That is the page to re-read if a rumor says the game slipped again.

## Pre-order and pre-load

[Rockstar's November 10 pre-load page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/pre-load-now) says pre-loading is available for people who have pre-ordered, and it tells anyone who has not pre-ordered that this is the time. Take-Two's June 24, 2026 release had listed pre-load for November 12. The newer Rockstar page is the one to follow this week: open the console store and see if your pre-order can download.

Purchases before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack, according to [Take-Two](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi/). Digital pre-orders also include one month of GTA+. The physical box contains a download code, not a disc.

## Pick an edition on price, not on a collector rumor

Standard is $79.99. Ultimate is $99.99 and adds premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and actions in Jason and Lucia's story. Take-Two did not announce a separate collector's edition. The comparison is on the [price page](/price).

## Read the story Rockstar actually printed

Jason and Lucia, Vice City, and the state of Leonida are summarized in Rockstar's own words on the [Lucia and Jason page](/characters/lucia) and the [map page](/map). Platforms, including the missing PC date, are on the [platforms page](/platforms).
