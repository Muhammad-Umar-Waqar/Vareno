## Why Mechanical Hard Drives (HDD) Are Obsolete for GTA 6

In earlier generation titles like *Grand Theft Auto V* (2013), game worlds were engineered around 5,400 RPM spinning platters reading at ~100 MB/s. 

GTA 6 eliminates this legacy constraint. Built natively for current-generation SSD throughputs delivering 5,500 MB/s uncompressed data bandwidth on PlayStation 5, Leonida dynamically streams 4K textures, volumetric clouds, interior environments, and high-density traffic without transition loading screens. 

Running GTA 6 from an HDD would cause catastrophic asset hitching, invisible buildings, and audio desynchronization. Microsoft DirectStorage API integration will be a prerequisite on Windows.

## Console Hardware Equivalence & PC Expectations

To gauge whether your current PC outpaces the launch platforms:

1. **PlayStation 5 Baseline:** Features 8 Zen 2 CPU cores clocked up to 3.5 GHz, an RDNA 2 GPU with 36 Compute Units yielding 10.28 TFLOPs, and 16GB of shared GDDR6 memory. In PC terms, this translates roughly to an **AMD Ryzen 7 3700X paired with an Nvidia GeForce RTX 2070 Super or RTX 3060**.
2. **Xbox Series X Baseline:** Features 8 Zen 2 CPU cores at 3.8 GHz and 52 Compute Units generating 12.15 TFLOPs. In PC terms, this roughly mirrors an **AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT or Nvidia RTX 3070**.
3. **PS5 Pro Target:** Boasts an upgraded RDNA 3/4 hybrid GPU with PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) and advanced ray-tracing hardware, aligning closely with an **Nvidia RTX 4070**.

PC hardware holding an RTX 3070 or better with 32GB RAM will match or surpass the launch console fidelity when Rockstar releases the Windows version.
