The thirteen-year wait is officially over. On November 19, 2026, Rockstar Games launched ***Grand Theft Auto VI*** worldwide on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, igniting a global cultural and economic earthquake that has dominated live newsrooms from NBC News and Bloomberg to The New York Times and IGN.

From an all-time record **99/100 Metacritic score** and **$1.84 billion in opening sales** to an official **Miami music festival** and **Twitch Drops**, here are the definitive live updates from the biggest entertainment release in human history.

## 2. Sensor Tower Telemetry: 19 Million Copies & $1.84 Billion in Revenue

According to comprehensive global data released by market intelligence firm **Sensor Tower**:
* **19+ Million Copies Sold:** By Thursday afternoon, global digital downloads across the PlayStation Store and Xbox Games Store combined with physical boxed discs surpassed 19 million units.
* **$1.84 Billion in Revenue:** The game easily eclipsed *Grand Theft Auto V*’s historic 2013 benchmark ($800 million in 24 hours), earning more in its first 36 hours than major Hollywood movie franchises earn across their entire cinematic lifetimes.
* **Pre-Order Records:** Global retail pre-orders had already locked in between $1.65 billion and $1.8 billion prior to midnight unlocks.

## 5. Global Cultural Phenomenon: Gamers Skip Work Worldwide

The cultural gravity of GTA 6 has reshaped everyday commerce:

* **Gen Z Locked In:** A nationwide poll by **Data for Progress** found that **44% of all 18-to-29-year-olds** planned to play *GTA VI* on day one, while an additional 40% personally knew someone skipping obligations to play.
* **Corporate Holidays:** Consumer tech brands and startups worldwide offered staff optional paid holidays on November 19. Indian electronics firm *boAt* (with 500+ employees) posted: *"In the spirit of work-life balance, we’re offering an optional day off on 19th November so that you can work hard on your missions."*
* **World Leaders Chime In:** Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi shared a viral mock-up of the game poster on X, declaring *"India is playing a whole new game!"* to highlight the 13-year economic progress of the nation between GTA V (2013) and GTA VI (2026).
* **Delivery Surge:** Quick-commerce grocery platforms like Blinkit and Zepto documented unprecedented rushes on snacks, energy drinks, and gaming peripherals.
